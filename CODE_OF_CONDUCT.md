# Capryos Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Capryos is committed to building a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community for everyone. This project exists to make crypto, business, and technology knowledge easier to understand and more accessible, and that mission depends on open collaboration grounded in professionalism, kindness, and mutual respect.

Contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to help create an environment where people of all backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives can participate safely and productively.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive community include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints, experiences, and skill levels.
- Giving and receiving feedback constructively.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.
- Being honest, clear, and professional in technical and non-technical discussions.
- Respecting maintainers' time, project scope, and contribution guidelines.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or hateful conduct of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting, derogatory, or personal attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Doxxing, sharing private information, or threatening another person.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions, pull requests, issues, or community spaces.
- Misrepresentation, spam, scams, or misleading promotional content.
- Posting content that is abusive, defamatory, obscene, or otherwise inappropriate.
- Any conduct that would be considered unprofessional or unsafe in a collaborative open source environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all Capryos project spaces, including but not limited to:

- GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and project boards.
- Documentation, comments, and review conversations.
- Social or community spaces officially associated with Capryos.
- Public spaces when an individual is representing Capryos or its community.

## Contribution Standards

To keep the project useful and trustworthy, contributors should:

- Submit work that aligns with the project's mission of making complex topics clear and actionable.
- Keep discussions relevant, evidence-based, and respectful.
- Avoid low-value promotional posts, misleading claims, and manipulative engagement tactics.
- Clearly disclose limitations, assumptions, or uncertainties when sharing technical, crypto, or business-related information.
- Prioritize clarity, accessibility, and accuracy in documentation and content.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, documentation, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they determine to be inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to the project maintainer by opening a private communication channel or by contacting:

- **Project contact:** hello@capryos.com

Reports should include as much relevant detail as possible, such as links, screenshots, timestamps, and a short description of the incident. All good-faith reports will be reviewed and handled as promptly and fairly as possible.

## Enforcement Guidelines

When responding to violations, maintainers may take actions including:

1. Warning the individual involved.
2. Removing or editing offending content.
3. Temporarily limiting participation.
4. Closing or locking discussions.
5. Suspending or permanently banning repeat or severe offenders.

The response will depend on the severity, context, and pattern of behavior.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct was written for the Capryos project with structure and expectations informed by common open source community standards and Netlify's requirement that open source projects prominently provide a code of conduct.
